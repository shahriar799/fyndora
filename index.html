Questions we hear most

What is the candle container made of?

It's a metal tin with a lid, printed with a decorative design. Once the candle is finished, you can keep using the tin.

How long does it burn?

At least 16 hours. On the first burn, let the top melt evenly all the way to the edge so the candle doesn't tunnel.

What are the candle's size and weight?

The candle is 6.9 cm wide and 7.7 cm tall, and the box is 7.2 × 8 cm. It holds 113 g of soy wax and weighs 150 g with the box. It's a small candle, just right for a bedside table, a desk or a gift.

How strong is the fragrance?

It's moderate, a soft scent for a small room rather than a large hall. If you prefer a strong fragrance, this candle may not be the right fit for you.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is ৳60 inside Lakshmipur and ৳120 everywhere else, including Dhaka. Our stock is in Lakshmipur and every parcel travels from there by Steadfast courier, so it costs a little more outside the district. You pay the delivery charge once per order, however many candles you buy.

What are the payment and delivery options?

We only take cash on delivery, so you pay when your parcel arrives. Orders go out by Steadfast courier and take 2 to 5 days to reach you.
