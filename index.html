Questions we hear most

What is the tin made of?

Metal. It is a painted tin jar with a lid, so it stays useful after the candle is finished.

How long does it burn?

At least 16 hours. Let the top melt evenly to the edge on the first burn so the candle does not tunnel.

How big is it, and how heavy?

The candle is 6.9 cm wide and 7.7 cm tall; the box is 7.2 × 8 cm. It holds 113 g of soy wax and weighs 150 g with the box. It is a small candle, best for a bedside, desk or a gift.

How strong is the fragrance?

Moderate. It gives a soft scent to a small room rather than filling a large hall. If you want a strong scent, this may not be the one for you.

Why is delivery ৳120?

Our stock is in Lakshmipur, so every parcel travels by Steadfast courier from there. Inside Lakshmipur it is ৳60; everywhere else, Dhaka included, it is ৳120.

How do I pay, and when will it arrive?

Cash on delivery only, so you pay when it reaches you. It takes 2 to 5 days.
